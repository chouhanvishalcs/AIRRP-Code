Capabilities

Six capabilities. One connected product.

Each capability is shown the same way: what it does, how AIRRP does it differently, and why that is useful. All six work from the same governed enterprise information, so findings, controls, evidence and decisions stay connected.

  • Context-aware work

    AIRRP starts from the organisation, processing, systems and relationships actually in scope.

  • Governed reuse

    The same approved facts, controls and evidence can support more than one workflow, where appropriate.

  • Traceable decisions

    You can see why something was selected, assessed or escalated, and who approved a material decision.

01

Compliance Management

What it does
Helps organisations identify relevant laws, regulations and frameworks, determine the requirements applicable to their context, and manage assessments against governed controls and evidence.
How it works differently
Reuses approved enterprise facts rather than starting from a generic questionnaire for every framework. Connects requirements to shared Master Controls, evidence expectations and explainable applicability decisions.
Why it’s useful
Less repeated interpretation and collection work; more consistent, traceable compliance assessments across programs.
  • Context-aware
  • Governed reuse
  • Traceable
Workflow
  1. Enterprise context
  2. Applicable requirements
  3. Governed controls and evidence
  4. Assessment and decisions
02

Risk Assessments

What it does
Supports assessment of business processes, AI applications, networks, servers, cloud environments and other in-scope business or technology subjects.
How it works differently
Uses the actual assessment subject and available enterprise context to select relevant controls, evidence and questions; links assessment findings to risk decisions instead of leaving assessments as isolated checklists.
Why it’s useful
More relevant assessments, clearer gaps, and an actionable connection from findings to risk management.
  • Context-aware
  • Traceable
Workflow
  1. Select subject
  2. Understand scope and dependencies
  3. Assess controls and evidence
  4. Findings and risk decisions
03

Centralised Enterprise Risk Register

What it does
Brings risks identified through different assessments and business activities into one enterprise risk register, with ownership, evaluation, treatment and tracking.
How it works differently
Preserves the connection between source findings, affected subjects, risk reasoning, human decisions and treatments rather than maintaining detached risk entries.
Why it’s useful
Better enterprise-wide visibility, less duplicate reporting and clearer accountability for risk decisions.
  • Traceable
Workflow
  1. Findings and risk inputs
  2. Governed risk assessment
  3. Owner/decision
  4. Treatment and tracking
04

Third-Party Risk Management

You assess your third parties
What it does
Supports third-party relationship understanding, qualification, risk assessment, due diligence, evidence review and risk tracking across the relationship lifecycle.
How it works differently
Uses known business context and existing enterprise knowledge where available, reducing repeated requests for information and connecting third-party risks to the enterprise view.
Why it’s useful
More contextual vendor risk decisions and less fragmented third-party assurance work.
  • Context-aware
  • Governed reuse
Workflow
  1. Identify governed third-party relationship
  2. Establish business context
  3. Assess and review
  4. Track risk and follow-up
05

Governance Document Intelligence

What it does
Helps create comprehensive policies, procedures, standards, baselines, plans and privacy notices.
How it works differently
Builds drafts using governed organisational facts and available regulatory/control context, followed by review and approval instead of producing generic, context-free templates.
Why it’s useful
Documents can be more consistent with the organisation’s actual environment, reducing drafting effort and rework.
  • Context-aware
  • Governed reuse
  • Traceable
Workflow
  1. Select document
  2. Use governed knowledge
  3. Draft
  4. Review and approve
06

Customer Security and Compliance Questionnaire Assistance

Your customers assess you
What it does
Helps organisations prepare responses to due-diligence, security, privacy and compliance questionnaires received from their own customers.
How it works differently
Draws on approved organisational facts, governance documents, controls and evidence already available in AIRRP, with human review of proposed responses.
Why it’s useful
Faster, more consistent customer assurance responses without repeatedly collecting the same information.
  • Governed reuse
  • Traceable
Workflow
  1. Receive questionnaire
  2. Reuse approved information
  3. Prepare responses
  4. Review and finalise
Two directions of assurance

Third-party risk and customer questionnaires are separate capabilities.

Third-Party Risk Management assesses your third parties. Questionnaire Assistance helps when your customers assess you. Each has its own workflow in AIRRP.

Why connected capabilities add value

Approved enterprise facts, controls and evidence are governed once in AIRRP. When your licence includes more than one capability, the same approved information can support several workflows instead of being collected again for each:

  • Compliance assessments
  • Risk assessments and the risk register
  • Governance documents
  • Customer questionnaire responses
