Website privacy notice — working draft

This explains how the AIRRP website’s Request Demo and Contact Us form is intended to handle your information. It is not yet the legally approved notice.

1. Operator and contact

The website is intended to be operated by DSR Digital Technology. Before launch: confirm the exact registered legal entity, business address and official grievance/privacy contact. Product enquiries may be emailed to airrp@dsrdt.com.

2. Information we collect

When you use the form we collect your name, work email, organisation, the request type you choose (Request Demo or Contact Us) and, if you add it, what you would like to discuss. We do not ask for customer evidence, health data, credentials, incident records or other sensitive information in this form. You are not subscribed to a newsletter.

If the online form is not active, the page opens your own email application instead. Your message is then handled under your mail provider’s and our mailbox arrangements.

3. Purpose and service providers

We use the information only to understand and respond to your request. The website uses Cloudflare Workers and Turnstile for delivery and abuse prevention, a transactional email service (currently configured for Resend) to send your request to the AIRRP team inbox and an acknowledgement to you, and, if approved, a customer-relationship system. Confirm the deployed providers, regions, data flows, terms and transfer assessments before enabling the form.

Turnstile receives the technical signals needed for its security check; website delivery and security services can process network identifiers. No optional analytics are used by this website’s code.

4. Retention, security and sharing

The website code does not store submissions in a database. After the security check it sends a plain-text email to the configured team inbox and a fixed acknowledgement to the address you entered. Email-provider logs, Cloudflare processing and mailbox retention depend on the final configuration. Set the retention period, authorised personnel and deletion process before publication.

5. Your rights

You may use the confirmed official contact channel to request access, correction, deletion or other applicable rights. The final notice must document identity verification, applicable deadlines, the grievance procedure, recipients and how rights differ by jurisdiction.

Operator completion and legal approval are required.

Confirm the registered legal name and address, grievance contact, hosting, email and security providers, regions and transfers, retention and deletion schedules, cookie behaviour, effective date and jurisdiction-specific legal approval.
